The Source of the Spread

Where the discounts actually come from.

Nobody sells a prime asset below value for fun. They do it because something else matters more than price: speed, silence, or a deadline. Our job is to be standing there, capital ready, when that moment arrives.

01

Bank-Pressured Sellers

Refinancing falls through, covenants tighten, the bank sets a date. The owner needs a clean, fast, certain buyer. Certainty is the currency, and it buys a discount.

02

Expiring Residency Deadlines

Visa plans change, relocations collapse, holding structures stop making sense. When a residency clock runs out, a property that took years to build gets sold in weeks.

03

Divorce & Partnership Splits

Two parties, one asset, zero desire to wait for the perfect price. Court timetables and settlement pressure produce some of the deepest discounts we see.

04

Probate Sales

Heirs in three countries, none of whom want to manage a villa on another continent. Executors want a documented, dependable close, not a bidding circus.

05

Developer Bulk Allocations

Developers quietly release blocks of units below list to trusted buyers before launch. The public sees the launch price. Our clients saw the earlier one.

06

Quiet Sales by Public Figures

Some sellers cannot afford a listing photo, let alone a for-sale sign. Discretion is a condition of the deal. In exchange for silence, the price moves.

Straight Answers

What serious buyers ask us first.

Why would a seller accept less than market value?

Because for a pressured seller, price is not the scarce thing: time and certainty are. A bank deadline, a court date or a visa expiry makes a clean buyer who closes in weeks worth more than a higher offer that might close in months, or not at all. We bring that certainty, and the spread is what it earns.

Is buying distressed property risky?

Less than most people assume, if the work is done. The discount itself is the safety margin: entering 10 to 18% under comparable value means the market has to move against you before you touch your own capital. And we survey everything: title, structure, service charges, arrears, tenancy status. If a file does not survive diligence, it does not reach you.

What do you charge for off-market access?

A buyer-side fee, disclosed in full before you commit to anything. No hidden margins, no double representation. Our economics are simple: we earn when you close well, which is why the negotiation is fought on your side of the table. The exact terms depend on your mandate; we set them out in the first consultation. Book it here.

How fast do I need to move?

Files circulate briefly: a typical decision window is about 48 hours. That does not mean deciding blind. It means arriving prepared: brief defined, financing pre-arranged, structure ready. Buyers who do the preparation with us before the file lands are the ones who win them.
