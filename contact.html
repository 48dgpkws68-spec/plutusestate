Before You Book

Asked on almost every first call.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. We earn on closings, not calls. The consultation is where we decide together whether we can add value; if we cannot, we say so in the first thirty minutes and you leave with a clearer picture of both markets. That is why 92% of our clients transact again or refer within 24 months: the relationship starts with honesty, not an invoice.

I am not ready to buy yet. Should I still book?

That is the perfect time to plan an entry. The investors who buy best are the ones who defined their strategy, financing and target areas before the right file appeared. Off-market and distressed opportunities circulate for days, sometimes hours; prepared buyers win them. Use the call to build your plan, then run your numbers in the Investor Suite while you wait for the right deal.

Can we speak Dutch or German?

Yes. The team covers English, Dutch, German and Spanish. Tell us your preference in the message field and we will match you with the right advisor. Contracts and legal documents are always reviewed in the language you are comfortable signing in.
