Residency, Handled

Buy the asset. Gain the base.

For many clients the property is also the residency plan. Both markets offer a route. We prepare the file alongside the purchase, so the two complete together.

United Arab Emirates

Dubai Golden Visa

Property of AED 2M or more (about €510,000) qualifies you for the 10-year renewable Golden Visa, family included. Add 0% personal income tax, 0% capital gains tax and no annual property tax, and the case makes itself. We handle the application with the purchase.

Spain

Non-Lucrative & Digital Nomad Visas

Spain closed its golden visa scheme in April 2025, so ignore anyone still selling it. The working routes today are the non-lucrative visa for passive-income residents and the digital nomad visa for remote workers. Our legal partners prepare both, in parallel with your Marbella purchase.

Rental Management

Own it from anywhere. We run it on the ground.

A rental property is a small business. Ours is making sure yours runs without you. One local team per market, one owner dashboard, one quarterly report that tells you the truth.

  • Tenant vetting. Income checks, references and deposit handling before anyone signs.
  • Dubai cheque handling. Rents are paid yearly upfront in 1 to 4 cheques. We collect, bank and chase them so you never do.
  • Marbella licensing & changeovers. VUT holiday-rental licence, guest registration, cleaning and key handovers between stays.
  • Maintenance. Vetted trades in both cities, pre-approved spend limits, photographed before and after.
  • Quarterly owner report. Income, costs, occupancy and current market value of the asset, in one document.

Typical fees: long-term management 5 to 8% of rent. Short-term operation 15 to 25% of revenue, depending on service level. Disclosed before you commit, like everything we charge.

Common Questions

Asked before. Answered straight.

Do you work with buyers remotely?

Yes, most of our clients start that way. We run live video viewings, send full written reports with comparables and service-charge history, and complete via power of attorney with our legal partners. Many investors have bought, furnished and rented a property with us before ever seeing it in person.

What does the advisory cost?

A flat or percentage fee, disclosed upfront and agreed in writing before we begin. In practice it is often covered by the discount we negotiate: our average across closings is 11.4% below asking. You will always know exactly what you pay and what you get, before you commit to anything.

Can you handle everything while I never fly in?

Yes. Sourcing, negotiation, legals via power of attorney, mortgage, renovation, furnishing, tenants and eventual resale can all run without you setting foot in Dubai or Marbella. Many clients buy remotely and visit their property for the first time as owners. Your dashboard and quarterly reports keep you fully informed in between.

Do you sell properties too?

Yes, for owners. Our exit advisory covers timing against live comparables, presentation, pricing and negotiation on the sell side. If you bought through us, we already track your asset and will tell you honestly when the market has done its work. If you bought elsewhere, we start with a valuation call.
