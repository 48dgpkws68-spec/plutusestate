The Thesis

Why Marbella never goes out of style.

Fashions rotate. The coast does not. Marbella has been the address of European wealth for six decades, and the fundamentals underneath it have only tightened.

  • Scarcity of prime land. The strip between the mountains and the sea is finite and largely built. Quality stock in the best zones is chronically short, and short supply is what holds prices through cycles.
  • Demand from 38 nationalities. Our own client base mirrors the market: buyers from across Europe, the Gulf and beyond, all competing for the same few streets. No single economy drives it, so no single economy can sink it.
  • The lifestyle capital of Europe. Golf, gastronomy, beach clubs, international schools, a marina full of reasons to stay. People do not just invest here. They live here, which keeps rental demand deep in every season.
  • Resilient through cycles. Prime Costa del Sol has compounded at roughly 7% a year since 2021 while other European luxury markets stalled. When money looks for a safe, sunny store of value, it looks here first.

The Marbella Play

Buy the worst villa on the best plot.

The coast is full of dated 1990s villas sitting on plots that could never be bought empty today. That mismatch is the single most reliable value play in this market.

  • Source under market. Tired interiors scare off lifestyle buyers. They do not scare us. We negotiate hard, our average across closings is 11.4% below asking.
  • Renovate at €1,000 to €1,600/m². Full architectural refits with contractors we have used for years, at cost levels we verify line by line.
  • Exit at 20 to 35% uplift. A renovated turnkey villa in a prime zone commands a premium the reno bill does not come close to consuming. That spread is your profit.

We run the entire play: sourcing, licence, build, staging and sale, or keep it and let peak-season rents pay for the works.

Questions

Marbella, answered straight.

The four questions every first-time Costa del Sol investor asks us. Anything deeper, ask a senior advisor directly.

Can non-EU citizens buy property in Marbella?

Yes, freely. Spain places no restrictions on foreign ownership. You need an NIE number, a Spanish bank account and a lawyer, all of which we arrange as part of the purchase. Buyers from outside the EU close here every week of the year.

When is the best time to buy?

November to February. The tourists are gone, listings sit longer and sellers who need to close before spring negotiate hardest. That off-season window is where our best below-asking results happen. If you want to buy well, start the conversation in autumn.

Is short-term rental legal in Marbella?

Yes, with a VUT licence, Andalusia's tourist rental registration. We confirm eligibility per property and community before you buy, then handle the registration itself. In peak zones a properly licensed property grosses 8 to 10% in the May to October season.

How long does a purchase take?

6 to 10 weeks is typical from accepted offer to notary, a little longer with a mortgage. With power of attorney you never need to fly in for the signing. Our record from first call to keys stands well inside that range.
