The Thesis

Why Marbella never goes out of style.

Fashions rotate. The coast does not. Marbella has been the address of European wealth for six decades, and the fundamentals underneath it have only tightened.

  • Scarcity of prime land. The strip between the mountains and the sea is finite and largely built. Quality stock in the best zones is chronically short, and short supply is what holds prices through cycles.
  • Demand from 38 nationalities. Our own client base mirrors the market: buyers from across Europe, the Gulf and beyond, all competing for the same few streets. No single economy drives it, so no single economy can sink it.
  • The lifestyle capital of Europe. Golf, gastronomy, beach clubs, international schools, a marina full of reasons to stay. People do not just invest here. They live here, which keeps rental demand deep in every season.
  • Resilient through cycles. Prime Costa del Sol has compounded at roughly 7% a year since 2021 while other European luxury markets stalled. When money looks for a safe, sunny store of value, it looks here first.

Plan Your Residency Route

From the UK

Buying property in Spain from the UK.

UK citizens can still buy property in Spain freely: Brexit changed nothing about ownership. You need an NIE tax number, a Spanish bank account and an independent lawyer, and you pay the same purchase taxes as a Spanish buyer. What did change: stays are capped at 90 days in any 180, and rental income is taxed at 24%.

01

NIE and bank account

The NIE is the foreigner's tax number every Spanish purchase needs. Apply at a Spanish consulate in the UK, or let a representative in Spain apply with your power of attorney. Then open a non-resident account at a Spanish bank and expect source-of-funds questions.

02

Check, then reserve

Your own lawyer checks title, charges, licences, community debts and the IBI receipts at the land registry and town hall. Only then do you sign the private contract (contrato de arras) and pay the deposit, usually 10% of the price.

03

Sign at the notary

The deed (escritura) is signed before a Spanish notary, in person or through a power of attorney. A UK power of attorney is signed before a UK notary and apostilled by the FCDO, or signed at a Spanish consulate. You pay the balance and the purchase taxes.

04

Register and file

The deed is registered at the land registry in your name. From then on you file a Spanish non-resident tax return (Modelo 210) for the property every year, let or not, and declare the income to HMRC at home.

Questions

Marbella, answered straight.

The questions every first-time Costa del Sol investor asks us. Anything deeper, ask a senior advisor directly.

Can non-EU citizens buy property in Marbella?

Yes, freely. Spain places no restrictions on foreign ownership. You need an NIE number, a Spanish bank account and a lawyer, all of which we arrange as part of the purchase. Buyers from outside the EU close here every week of the year.

When is the best time to buy?

November to February. The tourists are gone, listings sit longer and sellers who need to close before spring negotiate hardest. That off-season window is where our best below-asking results happen. If you want to buy well, start the conversation in autumn.

Is short-term rental legal in Marbella?

Yes, with a VUT licence, Andalusia's tourist rental registration. We confirm eligibility per property and community before you buy, then handle the registration itself. In peak zones a properly licensed property grosses 8 to 10% in the May to October season.

Can I buy property in Spain from the UK without flying in?

Yes. A representative can apply for your NIE and sign the deed for you under a power of attorney. A UK power of attorney is signed before a UK notary and apostilled by the FCDO, or signed at a Spanish consulate. Most UK buyers still visit once to see the property before they reserve it.

Does owning a home in Spain let me stay longer than 90 days?

No. Since Brexit, UK citizens can stay up to 90 days in any 180-day period without a visa, and property ownership does not extend that. The golden visa for property buyers ended on 3 April 2025, so longer stays need a Non-Lucrative Visa or a Digital Nomad Visa.

How long does a purchase take?

6 to 10 weeks is typical from accepted offer to notary, a little longer with a mortgage. With power of attorney you never need to fly in for the signing. Our record from first call to keys stands well inside that range.
