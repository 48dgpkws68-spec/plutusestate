Our Story

Founded by investors. Not by brokers.

This firm did not start with a shop window and a rack of listings. It started with our own capital, in the hardest market Dubai has ever seen.

After the 2009 correction, while most of the industry was leaving, we were buying: distressed units, bank pressure sales, owners who needed out in weeks. We learned what a building is really worth when nobody is pretending. That discipline never left us.

Clients came the only way that matters in this business: one investor telling another. When enough of them asked us to look at Europe, we opened the Marbella desk, pairing Dubai's yield with the Costa del Sol's legacy value. Two markets, one method.

Today Plutus Estate is a private advisory with offices in Dubai Marina and on the Marbella Golden Mile. We review 120+ off-market and distressed files a year, and we still walk every one of them the way we walked those first units in 2009: as if the money were our own.

  • Price is a fact, not a feeling. The correction taught us to price against real closings, never against asking prices or a broker's story.
  • Every building has a memory. Service charges, snag lists, rental history, who is selling and why. We read the file before we read the brochure.
  • The exit is planned at entry. We have never recommended a purchase without writing down how, when and to whom it sells.

How We Work

Three rules. No exceptions.

They cost us deals sometimes. They have never cost a client.

01

Discretion

Most of our book is never advertised. Sellers come to us because we do not turn their situation into a billboard, and buyers come to us because that is where the honest pricing lives.

What is said in a consultation stays in the consultation. No mailing lists, no portals, no leaks. In this segment, silence is a service.

02

Discipline

Models decide, not moods. Every file is run through the same math we publish in the Investor Suite: entry against real comparables, net yield after every fee, exit scenarios in writing.

If the numbers do not clear the bar, we pass, however beautiful the terrace. Our clients average 11.4% below asking because we walk away easily.

03

Alignment

Buyer-side only. We carry no seller mandates that quietly steer you toward stock we need to move. There is exactly one client in the transaction: you.

Fees are disclosed upfront, in writing, before you commit to anything. You always know whose side of the table we sit on.

The People

A small team, deliberately.

Access is personal. It does not scale like a call centre, and we have never tried to make it.

A senior team of advisors, analysts, renovation managers and licensed partners across both markets.

No junior salesfloor. No call scripts. The person who takes your first call is the person who negotiates your deal, and most of the team has bought and sold with their own money in the markets they cover. That changes how a file gets read.

Advisors sit in Dubai Marina and on the Marbella Golden Mile. Behind them: analysts who maintain building-by-building transaction data, renovation managers who price works before you offer, and licensed legal and mortgage partners in both jurisdictions who close what we start.

  • Senior advisors run your strategy and every negotiation personally.
  • Analysts keep live comparables on the buildings and urbanizaciones we cover.
  • Renovation managers cost the works before the offer, not after the surprise.
  • Licensed partners handle conveyancing, structuring and mortgages in both jurisdictions.
