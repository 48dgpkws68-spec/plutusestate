Buying Costs · Worked Out

What it costs to buy, line by line.

The same purchase price in Dubai and in Marbella, with every tax and fee written out. Three price points, resale and new build, no rounding into a vague percentage.

By Nick van Straaten · Updated 24 November 2026

The Short Answer

About 6% in Dubai. 9 to 13% in Marbella.

Dubai charges a 4% transfer fee, about 2% buyer agency and a fixed trustee fee. Andalusia charges 7% transfer tax on a resale, or 10% VAT plus 1.2% stamp duty on a new build, and the buyer pays notary, registry and lawyer. Below is what that means in euros.

After the Purchase

The costs that come every year.

Dubai

Annual service charges per square foot, set per building and community, with a wide spread between towers. No annual property tax and no personal income tax on rent. Read the service charge history of the exact building before you buy; we include it in every shortlist.

Marbella

Annual municipal property tax (IBI), community fees for the urbanisation, and Spanish non-resident income tax on rental income or on the deemed rental value when the home is not let. These depend on the property and your residency, so we model them per file with your adviser.

Before You Ask

Four questions buyers ask about costs.

How much does it cost to buy a €500,000 property in Dubai?

Roughly 6% on top of the price. On €510,000 (about AED 2 million) that is the 4% Dubai Land Department fee (€20,400), about 2% buyer agency (€10,200) and AED 4,200 trustee and admin (about €1,071): around €31,671 in total. Buying off-plan direct from a developer usually removes the agency line.

How much does it cost to buy a €500,000 property in Marbella?

For a resale home in Andalusia, 7% transfer tax (ITP) plus about 2% for notary, land registry and lawyer: around €45,900 on €510,000. A new build carries 10% VAT plus 1.2% stamp duty (AJD) plus the same fees: around €67,320.

Which is cheaper to buy into, Dubai or Marbella?

Dubai. On the same price the one-off costs are about 6% in Dubai against about 9% for a Marbella resale and about 13% for a Marbella new build. The gap grows with the price because almost every line is a percentage.

Are these the final costs?

They are planning figures based on the published tax rates and typical fees. Mortgage costs, valuation, furniture, annual service charges in Dubai and annual property tax (IBI) and community fees in Marbella come on top. Tax rates can change, so confirm them with a local lawyer before you sign.
